# Does ProManAI need login? (SCRUM-22)

**Status: open decision. This note frames the options; the decision and rationale get recorded here once the team picks one.**

## Options

1. **Fully public, no sign-in.** Anyone can practise immediately; nothing to create or remember. Fits the prototype phase and a "try it first" product. Costs: no saved history across devices, no personal streaks/XP that survive a browser reset, no way to attribute shared experiences or peer mocks to a person.
2. **Accounts with login.** Needed for durable personal progress, peer mock booking, and sharing experiences under a name. Costs: signup friction, email verification, password/account handling, more to build and secure.
3. **No-sign-in now, accounts later.** Start public; add accounts only when a feature genuinely needs identity (peer mocks and sharing are the first candidates).

## Current prototype behavior

The prototype demonstrates option 1 in spirit: a demo "Login" button enters the full experience with a sample account, and no real authentication exists. Nothing in the prototype requires real accounts.

## Points from the team calls

- Nothing in the Sep-Oct 2026 recordings requires auth yet.
- The three-pillar direction (Oct 10) emphasizes fast concept iteration without technical blockers; sign-in is exactly that kind of blocker for reviewers.
- Peer mock interviews and shared experiences eventually need some notion of identity, whichever option is chosen.
