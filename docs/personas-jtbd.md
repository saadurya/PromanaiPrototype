# ProManAI personas and jobs-to-be-done (SCRUM-12)

Portfolio documentation for the ProManAI concept prototype. Grounded in the team's ideation doc and the Sep-Oct 2026 call recordings. Status: draft for team review.

## Primary persona: the APM candidate

**Who.** A beginner preparing for Associate Product Manager interviews - typically a final-year student or early-career switcher, no prior PM title. The team is its own target user: everyone on the team is currently preparing for these interviews.

**Goals.**
- Practise answering PM interview questions out loud, the way a real interview feels.
- Find out exactly where their answers are weak, and fix those gaps before the real interview.
- Walk into interviews knowing what companies actually ask.

**Pains.**
- No human interviewer available on demand; friends cannot always play that role.
- Prep material is scattered across the internet and hard to prioritize with limited time.
- Reading questions is not the same as answering under time pressure.
- Hard to know which AI tools and topics actually matter for PM work.

**Jobs to be done.**
1. When I have 20 minutes, let me run a realistic spoken mock interview alone, so I can practise every day without needing another person.
2. When I finish a mock, show me my strengths and gaps, and point me at the exact resources that close each gap, so my prep time goes to the right place.
3. When I target a company or industry, let me practise with questions and scenarios from that industry, so the practice matches the real interview.
4. When I want to know what real interviews look like, let me read actual interview experiences with expert rankings, so I learn the bar before I am measured against it.
5. When I want human practice, match me with a peer who is free at the same time and give us suggested questions, so the mock runs itself.
6. When I plan my prep week, give me a dated roadmap from my gaps, so I always know what to study next.

## Secondary persona: the practising PM

**Who.** A working PM preparing for senior loops or keeping interview-ready.

**Goals.** Stay sharp on strategy and behavioral rounds; benchmark against expert-ranked answers.

**Jobs to be done.** Higher-difficulty mocks in specific categories; contributing their own interview experiences and expert rankings back to the library.
